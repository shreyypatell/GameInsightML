What this project does

Two ML tasks, five models, one dataset

Regression

Three different regressors take a crack at estimating exactly how many million copies a title will sell.

Classification

Two classifiers sort games into Low, Medium, and High sales brackets instead of guessing an exact number.

Exploratory Analysis

Dig into genre, platform, and publisher patterns across decades of releases through interactive charts.

Manual Prediction

Type in a hypothetical game's details and get a sales estimate back instantly from any of the trained models.

Batch Prediction

Drop in a CSV of upcoming titles and pull down predictions for the entire list in one go.
